## 4. Customers and initial revenue

ASSUMPTION: US-based fulfillment, USD, domestic shipping first; global educational access. Global physical commerce expands by region after shipping review.

| Priority/customer | Journey and purchase | Proposed price band | Revenue role |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1: Adult maker / independent experimenter | Starter guide → kit → measured experiment → refill | $39–59 starter; $19–35 refill | Early direct checkout and repeat demand |
| 1: Educator / college lab | Lesson preview → teacher pilot → class pack → semester refills | $149–229 for 12 students in 6 pairs | Larger orders; allow quotations and purchase-order requests |
| 2: Researcher / developer | Paper or material → documented grade → sample → larger order | $15–60 samples; $75–250 bundles | Documentation-led repeat materials revenue |
| 2: Professional | Scoped intake → specialist consultation → review/project | $125–200/30 min; $300–750 scoped review | Early service revenue, limited by available expertise |
| Later: Manufacturer | Qualification → sourcing quote → supply agreement | Quoted | Expand only after supply and quality systems work |

RECOMMENDATION: Start with kits + refills + selected raw materials, alongside a small vetted consulting roster. Free knowledge drives discovery. Delay membership, marketplace commissions and large courses until repeat usage demonstrates a need. A consultation price is a proposed client fee, not retained platform revenue; deduct specialist payment and administration. Do not promise access before experts commit.
