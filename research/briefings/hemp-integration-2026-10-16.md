# Hemp integration with geopolymer binders

16 October 2026 (Eastern). Research checkpoint; OpenAlex remains on hold.

The useful split is between **hemp shiv/hurd as lightweight aggregate**, **short
bast fibers as reinforcement**, and **fabric/textile assemblies**. Each needs
its own mix accounting and test program. This batch identifies direct geopolymer
hempcrete precedents, follows durability evidence, and revisits existing foam,
biochar and pilot gaps. It does not establish a transferable building recipe.

## Other hemp reinforcement research

**Durability and treatment.** [Poletanovic, Kopecsko and Merta (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.conbuildmat.2024.137957)
studied hornification in fly-ash geopolymer mortar. The [institutional abstract](https://repositum.tuwien.at/handle/20.500.12708/204863)
distinguishes laboratory ageing from wet/dry exposure; heating during accelerated
ageing can strengthen the matrix while energy absorption declines. Retrieve the
complete methods before comparing treatment conditions or claiming service life.

[Poletanović's 2025 dissertation](https://doi.org/10.34726/hss.2025.136875)
is a useful publication map. Its [repository abstract](https://repositum.tuwien.at/handle/20.500.12708/220431)
contrasts fiber pretreatments with composite carbonation: strength gains can
accompany embrittlement. The repository marks it “In Copyright.” No thesis text
was retained or extracted. The thesis and associated papers may reuse datasets;
they must not be counted as independent corroboration.

**Additional extraction targets.** [Hemp/red-mud/fly-ash composites (2021)](https://doi.org/10.3390/ma14030511)

## Three proposed experiments—not literature results

1. **Shiv/binder compatibility screen.** First recover a complete direct-shiv
   protocol above. Compare its binder control with the documented shiv series;
   keep shiv grading, moisture conditioning, binder chemistry, compaction and
   cure fixed. Record absorbed versus added water, working time, dry density,
   compression and thermal conductivity at controlled moisture. Dose selection
   remains pending; reject a candidate that cannot meet the chosen handling,
   density or moisture criteria. No structural use is inferred.
2. **Fiber treatment versus toughness retention.** Use unreinforced, untreated
   fiber and documented treated-fiber groups at matched fiber content and length.
   Compare same-age laboratory controls with wet/dry specimens. Measure the full
   load–deflection response, not just peak bending strength; track mass, water
   absorption and cracking. A treatment fails the intended toughness objective
   if strength improves but retained energy absorption becomes unacceptable.
3. **Shiv core with reinforced geopolymer skin.** This is an untested assembly
   proposal in this batch. Compare core alone, unreinforced skin and hemp-fiber
   skin; separately test bond and differential shrinkage under moisture cycling.
   A fiber result from dense mortar cannot establish adhesion to a porous shiv
   core. Skin thickness and doses await compatible source methods. Debonding,
   trapped moisture or cracking would rule out the assembly as formulated.

Choose application-specific acceptance thresholds and independent specimen
replication before testing; repeated instrument readings are not replicates.
No numerical performance predictions, optimum doses or combined benefits are
asserted. Review was by one AI agent, not a human/domain expert.
