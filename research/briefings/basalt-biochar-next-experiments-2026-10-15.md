# Basalt and hardwood biochar: next experiments

15 October 2026 · AI-assisted research planning, not a validated recommendation.
These two papers are different datasets with different matrices. Recipe variants
within each paper are not independent replication. Public recipe facts have their
own audit manifests; the proposals below have not been tested here.

## Reproduce the hardwood-biochar comparison first

Question: does the published dose response persist with the actual materials
available to the user? Start by specifying metakaolin grade, biochar feedstock,
pyrolysis, sieve size, moisture and activator solution composition. A substitute
creates a new experiment, not a reproduction of the source recipe.

[Saccani et al.](https://doi.org/10.3390/ma18030486), Tables 2 and 4, provide a
control and four replacement levels (1, 2, 5 and 8% of original metakaolin mass).
These are not wet-batch percentages. Compare compression at the same reported
ages and measure workability separately. Higher-dose controls matter because
strength is not monotonic. The original design keeps activator mass constant as
metakaolin decreases: binder chemistry and biochar dose therefore change together.
A later chemistry-controlled series would answer a different question and needs
qualified assays before specifying its quantities.

Unresolved before numerical comparison: initial compression replicate count,
reported spread definition, exact cure temperature, NaOH solution density/water,
and equivalence of the available material grades. Record independent batches and
specimens separately; do not count repeated instrument readings as replication.

## Separate fiber length from dose

[Le et al.](https://doi.org/10.3390/polym13050751), Table 3, reports the complete
matrix series now linked on the recipe pages. Hold dose constant to compare
lengths, and length constant to compare doses. Dose is relative to geopaste,
not all mortar. The source’s mixing order and sand grading belong in the trial
record. Check dispersion and casting behavior alongside strength. Matrix prisms
cannot establish the performance of textile-reinforced plates or adhesion to wood.

## Untested combination: biochar plus chopped basalt

Question: can a selected biochar-containing matrix accept dispersed basalt while
retaining useful workability? Neither paper establishes this combination. The
commercial geocement mortar and Argicem paste are not interchangeable matrices.

First qualify one base matrix and each additive separately. Then compare matched
baseline, biochar-only, basalt-only and combined trials, with equal cure, specimen
geometry and measurement method. Combined doses remain undecided: do not transfer
a geopaste-based fiber percentage directly into a replacement-based biochar recipe.
Track failed mixing, agglomeration and absent setting as outcomes. Any compression,
flexure, shrinkage or substrate-adhesion target requires its own measurement; no
numeric prediction or structural suitability follows from these two studies.
