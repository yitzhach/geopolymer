   discovery leads. Test an unchanged coating control against one qualified
   additive under matched substrate preparation, thickness, humidity and age.
   Measure application behavior, cracking and pull-off failure location as well
   as force. Bulk printed-concrete performance cannot establish coating behavior.
   Hypothesis type: untested transfer; no expected numerical improvement assigned.
2. **Does biochar processing change the shrinkage–strength tradeoff within a
   fixed binder?** Compare a no-char control with characterized char variants,
   keeping binder chemistry and accountable water fixed. Start with the two
   biochar candidates; retrieve full methods before selecting doses or processing
   ranges. Track shrinkage, workability and strength together. Raw grounds,
   pyrolysis char and combustion ash must remain separate material identities.
   Hypothesis type: condition-dependent material comparison; effects unresolved.
3. **Would adding a water-retaining material help or harm a perlite composite?**
   This connects the perlite and biochar leads as an untested combination. If
   source review justifies proceeding, compare baseline, perlite-only,
   biochar-only and combined variants under common curing/exposure. Measure
   density, water uptake, strength, shrinkage and thermal conductivity rather
   than using low density as proof of insulation. Determine quantities, sample
   size, test ages and acceptance limits before preparing specimens.
