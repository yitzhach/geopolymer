## What to develop next

1. **Hemp versus basalt for lightweight panels.** Use the phosphate-waste foam
   study as the first extraction target because both fiber types share an
   experimental matrix. Extract controls, density, moisture state, bending and
   conductivity together. Resolve the WP/S1 naming discrepancy first. Coating
   differences prevent attributing every difference solely to fiber type.
2. **Basalt plus carbon textile for thin panels.** Complete the source-version
   check and extract the matrix and laminate as separate designs. Retain loading
   direction, textile placement, impact geometry and workability observations.
   This is a direct hybrid precedent, not proof that arbitrary carbon powders
   or biochar will have the same effect.
3. **Fiber plus biochar is an untested connection here.** A future experiment
   would compare a matched control, fiber-only, biochar-only and their combination.
   Select doses only after full methods review. Fix binder, cure, test age and
   effective water; characterize biochar feedstock, pyrolysis, particle size and
   moisture uptake. Measure flow, density, shrinkage, bending and compression.
   Stop or revise a design on unacceptable dispersion or strength loss; thresholds
   and replication require application-specific selection. No combined benefit
   or predicted strength is asserted.

These are three distinct inspected papers, not three independently reproduced
results. The basalt/carbon paper also compares earlier work; that comparison is
not counted as a new independent dataset. No cross-study strength ranking,
structural qualification, generic carbon-negative claim or shear-strength
