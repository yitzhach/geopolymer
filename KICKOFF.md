## 1. Baseline — owner defined

Global platform for geopolymers and alkali-activated materials: Learn → Formulate → Buy → Test → Ask → Share. Education, material database, research summaries, formulations, commerce, tools, expert services; community later. Serve educators, students, makers, researchers, professionals, product developers and manufacturers. Modern scientific credibility with approachable UX. Connect knowledge to exact grades, experiments and purchasing. Never label a literature formulation independently validated without testing it.

Owner's public MVP target: 20–30 articles; 15–25 material pages; 10–20 products/raw materials; 3–5 kits; 10–20 formulations; 25–50 research summaries; strong search and checkout. Forum is not a dependency.
