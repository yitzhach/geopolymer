## 5. Initial catalog and kits

RECOMMENDATION: One repeatable metakaolin system powers three launch kit offers. Add a fourth comparison kit after validation. Below are product concepts, not ready-to-use chemical recipes.

| Offer | Proposed contents / outcome | Target price | Target fully packed product COGS |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Starter | One characterized precursor, compatible prepared activator, measured consumables; 2–3 small specimens and observation worksheet | $49 | ≤$20 |
| Classroom | Six paired workstations for 12 students; common experiment, teacher guide, data sheets and refill list | $179 | ≤$72 |
| Casting / Sculpture | Same qualified chemistry, appropriate filler and surface experiment; simple casting workflow | $69 | ≤$28 |
| Refill | Matched precursor/activator quantities, minimal consumables | $29 | ≤$12 |
| Later: Comparison | Two characterized precursor grades, compatible activator and controlled comparison protocol | $89 | ≤$36 |

COGS ceilings are design targets, not quotes. Include ingredients, inbound freight, packaging, labeling, assembly labor, QC and normal loss. Proposed kit product gross margin about 59–60%; raw material target 40–55%. Outbound freight, payment costs, support, returns and acquisition still reduce contribution.

Illustrative $49 kit: $20 COGS + $1.75 payment cost assumption + $3 pick/pack + $3 support/returns allowance = $21.25 contribution before advertising and fixed costs, with customer-paid shipping. Subsidizing $9 shipping reduces it to $12.25. No free-shipping promise until basket economics support it.

Start raw-material range with 10–12 qualified products: two metakaolin grades, one calcined clay, one slag, one sodium silicate, one potassium silicate, wollastonite, one compatible fiber, one microsphere grade and two pigments. Qualification may shrink the launch range. Offer 1 lb and 5 lb for appropriate powders; 10/25 lb selectively or by quote. Use smaller gram packs for additives/fibers and suitable sealed sizes for liquids. Store quantities in metric, displaying both systems accurately.

Defer fly ash until source/lot consistency is established; defer refractory, coating and fiber-performance kits until application testing supports them. Do not imply refractory certification, structural suitability, food contact or fire rating. Casting provides an initial application closely aligned with the owner's art practice without making the whole brand a craft store.

Kit definition before sale: exact grades and masses; specimen count; equipment/PPE supplied vs required; operator level; mixing and cure instructions; documented outcome; storage/shelf life; cleanup/disposal guidance; support limits; COGS; packaging and shipment classification. For classrooms, prioritize trained instructors and supervised older students, with age suitability established through review. Prepared activator or just-add-water packaging does not itself establish safety.

## 6. Sourcing and launch gates

RECOMMENDATION: Qualify an existing documented system before inventing a broad proprietary range. Investigate GPI as both competitor and potential reseller/distribution lead; Wöllner as manufacturer lead; Fishstone as retail benchmark or pilot procurement lead, not assumed wholesale source. No suppliers contacted and no reseller rights confirmed.

Request prices at bag/case, pallet and larger tiers; MOQ; lead time; freight to Kentucky; resale/repack permissions; SDS/TDS/COA; chemical composition and variability; particle size; liquid solids/density/modulus; shelf life and storage. Compare landed and repacked costs, not headline bulk price. Maintain grade and lot traceability and retain samples. Qualify substitutions experimentally.

VERIFIED: US DOT places classification, packaging and hazard-communication responsibilities on the shipper [S11]; OSHA Hazard Communication covers relevant chemical hazard classification, labels and SDS [S12]. Actual duties depend on product, activity and customer channel. This is a gate list, not a completed compliance determination.

Before physical launch: product-specific hazard and transport review; relevant consumer/classroom labeling review; carrier acceptance and shipping prices; suitable packaging; repacking workplace controls; product liability coverage; qualified expert review of kit instructions. International REACH/CLP and destination requirements remain later regional work. Do not assume silicates, powders or small quantities are exempt.

Demand pilot recommendation: interview 5 educators, 5 makers and 3 researchers; cost actual packed samples; run instructor/adult trials; seek 10 paid pilot orders once products are release-ready. Measure successful completion, support minutes, packed margin, freight as share of order and refill intent before expanding stock. Numbers are proposed validation targets, not a forecast.
