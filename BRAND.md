## 2. Naming and positioning

RECOMMENDATION: Choose a short master brand with a descriptive subtitle: “Geopolymer materials, methods & kits.” Use clear category words in page titles for search. Avoid promising environmental or performance benefits through the name.

| Direction | Candidate | Domain to investigate | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Modern laboratory | AlkaliLab | alkalilab.com | Preferred brand direction: short, technical, expandable; “alkali” needs explanation for beginners |
| Practical specialist | Geopolymer Works | geopolymerworks.com | Clearest category fit; longer, less distinctive, somewhat industrial |
| Knowledge and methods | Mineral Method | mineralmethod.com | Strong tone but existing commerce/domain history surfaced; deprioritize |
| Descriptive commerce | Geopolymer Supply | geopolymersupply.com | Deprioritize: domain resolves in search and Australian company of similar name exists |
| Laboratory shorthand | BinderLab | binderlab.com | Reject shortlist: active neuroscience site at exact .com and separate binder retailer |

VERIFIED: BinderLab exact domain is used [S5]; Mineral Method has indexed commerce history [S6]; Geopolymer Supply domain and similarly named Australian company appear [S7–S8].

LIMIT: Exact-name/domain searches did not surface a clear matching business for AlkaliLab or Geopolymer Works. This establishes neither registration availability nor trademark clearance. No registrar, RDAP, trademark or social-handle clearance completed. Before adoption: live registrar check, jurisdiction-specific trademark review including similar names, business-name and handle checks. No domain purchased.

RECOMMENDATION: Position around reproducible small-scale experimentation: documented grades, understandable methods, matched materials and expert help. Treat the underserved-classroom opportunity as a hypothesis to test, not proof of market size.
