VERIFIED = sourced fact; ASSUMPTION = unvalidated input; RECOMMENDATION = proposal. See DECISIONS.md for approval status.

## 8. Content structure and templates

Separate material family, supplier grade, supplier lot and purchasable SKU. Link through stable IDs: material ↔ grade ↔ product; paper ↔ formulation; formulation ↔ grade and test record; expert ↔ specialty. A similar material is not an exact substitute.

Material template: identity, role, chemistry, measured properties with sources, advantages/limits, handling, commercial grades, papers, methods and available products.

Research template: citation/DOI, publication/review status, full text vs abstract-only access, objective, exact materials and grades, formulation/basis, activator, mixing, cure, tests, results/units/uncertainty, limitations, practical interpretation, links and reviewer/date. Missing data stays “not reported”; do not fill it by inference. Clearly separate reported findings from editorial interpretation.

Mix template: source and version, evidence status, ingredients/grades/masses, ratio definitions, solution solids/water accounting, mixing sequence, cure and specimen geometry, test age/method, results, equipment, hazards, substitutions and bill of materials. Evidence badges: literature-reported; internally reproduced; independently tested, with scope and date. “Tested” is not certification.

Product template: exact grade, size/price/stock, intended experiment, what's included, required equipment, compatibility evidence, SDS/TDS/COA, lot information, shipping eligibility/cost, linked methods and refill options. Guest checkout; no required account to read methods or purchase.
